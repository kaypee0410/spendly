Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Your financial information is personal. This policy explains what Spendly collects, why, and how we look after it.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what we need to run the service:

  • Account details — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense records — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Basic technical data — such as session cookies needed to keep you signed in.

We do not collect bank account numbers, card details, or connect to your financial institutions.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to:

  • Show your expenses, category breakdowns, and summaries.
  • Keep your account secure and let you sign in.
  • Respond to support requests you send us.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in our database and protected with reasonable technical safeguards. Passwords are never stored in plain text. You can edit or delete your expenses at any time, and if you close your account, your personal data will be deleted.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on a small number of trusted providers, such as hosting services, to operate. These providers process data only on our behalf and only as needed to run the service. We do not share your expense data with third parties for their own use.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want to request access to or deletion of your data, contact us at privacy@spendly.example.
